Grandma Runs the Mafia Now
After a mafia boss dies, everyone expects his terrifying son to inherit the empire—but the will gives everything to his sweet 78-year-old mother.
She turns organized crime into a brutally efficient business using cookies, guilt trips, WhatsApp groups, and extremely polite threats.


A lazy unemployed man accidentally goes viral after someone films him sitting silently on a bench for eight hours.
People start calling him a philosopher, brands offer millions, and he must desperately hide the fact that he was only waiting because he lost his bus ticket.


Aliens Think Our Reality TV Is Real
Aliens secretly study Earth through reality television and conclude that humanity is ruled by attractive people screaming near swimming pools.
When they invade, they demand to negotiate exclusively with reality stars, forcing governments to pretend contestants are world leaders.


The Bank Robbers Who Forgot the Money
Four idiots execute the perfect bank robbery, escape flawlessly, and only realize hours later that they left the bags of money inside the bank.
Too embarrassed to admit it, they return disguised as customers while the police are actively investigating their own crime.


The Assassin Who Has Social Anxiety
The world’s deadliest assassin can eliminate anyone—but becomes completely useless when required to make small talk.
His hardest mission begins when he must infiltrate a wedding and realizes killing the target is easier than surviving the seating plan.


The Fake Psychic Who Starts Being Right
A scam artist pretends to speak with the dead and invents ridiculous predictions for desperate clients.
Then every prediction begins coming true—including one joke prophecy saying he will be murdered by a man wearing nothing but Crocs.


The Broke Guy Who Accidentally Became a Sugar Daddy
A man lies on a dating app and claims he is a millionaire, then somehow attracts a woman who is actually richer than him and thinks his poverty is “mysterious billionaire behavior.”
He keeps inventing increasingly stupid explanations for why he lives with his mother, takes the bus, and owns exactly three shirts.


The Gold-Digger Olympics
A mysterious billionaire invites twenty professional gold diggers to compete for marriage and a $1 billion inheritance.
The contestants slowly discover the billionaire is actually broke—and one of the gold diggers secretly owns the entire mansion.


My Clone Is Better Than Me
A depressed office worker buys an illegal clone to do his job, exercise, and handle his relationship while he plays games all day.
Within weeks, his girlfriend, parents, boss, and friends prefer the clone—and the clone decides the original is the only unnecessary person left.


The Devil Opens a Dating App
The Devil launches a dating app where every user is matched with the person most likely to ruin their life.
It becomes the world’s biggest app, but Satan panics when the algorithm gives him a 100% match with a broke single mother who thinks he’s pathetic.
