## VIDEO 01 — The Vanishing Salmon

* **Hook:** The manifest lists forty salmon crates. The locked dock cage holds thirty-nine.
* Sable brokered the shipment, so both her commission and reputation are at risk.
* The seals are intact, the cameras show nothing unusual, and the two guards contradict each other about everything except the locked cage.
* Everyone assumes an impossible overnight theft.
* Sable compares the documents from both ends of the shipment.
* **TWIST 1:** The original loading ticket also lists only thirty-nine crates.
* Nothing vanished from the cage. Someone created a fake fortieth crate on the invoice.
* The receiving clerk who approved forty crates has suddenly taken paid leave.
* Sable corners him in a cheap restaurant.
* He admits that an unknown organization called **the Drop** paid him to approve the false quantity.
* A small silver cup had been left in the cage as proof that the arrangement was complete.
* **TWIST 2:** The missing crate resurfaces at a Vulpine Cartel restaurant, where the Cartel unknowingly buys its own salmon back at triple price.
* The scheme is not ordinary theft. It is a private tax imposed through controlled scarcity.
* **STANDALONE PAYOFF:** Sable exposes the restaurant manager and recovers her commission.
* **SERIES CLUE:** An unsigned reorder form appears on her desk with exact payment and one sentence:
  **“Your accounting has improved.”**

## VIDEO 02 — The Wolf’s Paranoia

* **Hook:** Knuckles wins a brutal street fight, claims a new block and abandons it before sunrise.
* The Lupine Pack’s hidden food cache beneath that block has already been emptied.
* Knuckles wants an enemy he can attack, but no rival gang claims responsibility.
* His crew mocks him for ordering patrols against noises inside the walls.
* Then his prized bone disappears from his locked den.
* It returns the following night, polished and placed neatly on the highest shelf.
* Knuckles installs bells, wires and pressure traps.
* By morning, nothing has triggered, but every trap has been rebuilt more efficiently.
* **TWIST 1:** The only object actually missing is his territorial map.
* The intruder was not robbing him. It was studying how the Pack moves.
* A Vulpine defector reveals that the same thing happened in several fox-controlled districts.
* Soon afterward, the Pack’s meat deliveries stop.
* Knuckles controls more territory than ever but has less food.
* His map returns with every district redrawn according to warehouses, docks and delivery routes rather than gang borders.
* **TWIST 2:** Knuckles understands that his street victories are meaningless because someone else controls the supply network beneath them.
* **STANDALONE PAYOFF:** He abandons a planned gang war and redirects his soldiers to protect the remaining food routes.
* “You can’t punch a supply chain,” one lieutenant tells him.
* Knuckles punches the wall anyway.
* **SERIES CLUE:** Three polite taps answer from behind it.

## VIDEO 03 — The Syndicate’s Code

* **Hook:** Bastet opens an intercepted Drop expecting contraband and finds only a wax-sealed square covered in numbers.
* The Feline Syndicate is losing control of the luxury fish market.
* Her codebreakers spend a week trying to decipher the numbers.
* Bastet solves them in less than an hour.
* They are not a secret message. They are delivery times.
* **TWIST 1:** The schedule belongs to the Syndicate, but Bastet never approved it.
* Her captains have been secretly following the anonymous schedule because its routes are faster, safer and cheaper.
* Bastet issues a deliberately inefficient order to expose whoever is disobeying her.
* Every captain follows the anonymous instructions instead.
* One lieutenant apologizes—not to Bastet, but to the document.
* Her spies steal similar schedules from the Vulpine Cartel and Lupine Pack.
* Each gang has received different routes.
* **TWIST 2:** The three schedules fit together perfectly, preventing rival gangs from reaching the same dock at the same time.
* Someone has quietly reduced gang violence by managing their movements.
* **STANDALONE PAYOFF:** Bastet uses the schedule to prevent an ambush and save an entire shipment.
* She realizes the Syndicate does not have a rival.
* It has an unauthorized manager.
* **SERIES CLUE:** A final schedule slides through a vent, blank except for Bastet’s name and tomorrow’s date.

## VIDEO 04 — The Detective’s Dead End

* **Hook:** Eleven food markets collapse in one week, and Inspector Silas has no crime to investigate.
* There are no break-ins, threats, forged signatures or suspicious transfers.
* Every missing shipment was legally purchased at a fair price.
* **TWIST 1:** The markets were not robbed. Someone simply bought everything before ordinary customers could.
* Forty different companies made the purchases.
* Silas discovers that the companies share the same registration handwriting, the same accountant and sequential cash serial numbers.
* One source of money is operating behind dozens of legal identities.
* Silas follows a prepaid catering van carrying one of the purchases.
* Its paperwork is perfect.
* The driver has a signed collection order and no idea who owns the goods.
* Three guards claim they watched the food leave in daylight.
* **TWIST 2:** The guards are telling the truth. Every transfer happened openly, legally and on schedule.
* Silas has been searching for a thief when he should have been searching for a buyer.
* The shipments all converge on an abandoned municipal facility known as **Depot Seven**.
* **STANDALONE PAYOFF:** Silas freezes several shell accounts and temporarily restores food to two markets.
* **SERIES CLUE:** The seized accounting records contain hundreds of tiny payments to butchers, drivers, clerks and former gang members.

## VIDEO 05 — The Unholy Alliance

* **Hook:** Knuckles and Bastet, sworn enemies, wait together on a rooftop to ambush a midnight delivery truck.
* They believe the vehicle is supplying the organization manipulating both gangs.
* The truck arrives exactly on time.
* They stop it and find the driver asleep but unharmed.
* Nothing has been stolen.
* Instead, the cargo has been reorganized, damaged crates repaired and the route sheet improved.
* Knuckles considers this personally insulting.
* **TWIST 1:** The truck’s manifest already includes their ambush as a scheduled twenty-minute delay marked **“Inspection.”**
* Their supposedly secret plan was expected.
* The driver remembers finding coffee at the previous checkpoint with his name written on the cup.
* Knuckles and Bastet test for a traitor by giving three different plans to three lieutenants.
* None of the fake plans reaches the enemy.
* Bastet examines the gang’s ration tins and finds tiny variations in the batch stamps.
* **TWIST 2:** Their own supply requests contain encoded information about troop positions, schedules and planned attacks.
* They have been reporting their movements every time they order food.
* **STANDALONE PAYOFF:** Bastet changes the code and uses it to cancel an imminent ambush against both gangs.
* “We aren’t being watched,” she says. “We’re being processed.”
* **SERIES CLUE:** An unsigned note arrives:
  **“THE ARCHITECT REQUESTS YOUR COOPERATION.”**

## VIDEO 06 — The Great Hunger

* **Hook:** Gang-controlled broadcasters announce a citywide protein shortage.
* Within hours, meat prices triple and gang members begin fighting over scraps.
* Sable notices something strange: independent kitchens, orphanages, street animals and civilian neighborhoods still have food.
* Only markets controlled by the three major gangs are empty.
* The shortage is not citywide.
* It is targeted.
* Sable follows a well-fed stray through back alleys to a grate too small for her to enter.
* The route eventually leads to Depot Seven.
* The yard contains the gangs’ missing food, carefully sorted by district.
* Silent workers prevent looters from entering.
* **TWIST 1:** The guards are not mercenaries.
* They are butchers, clerks, drivers and former gang members who joined the Architect’s network in exchange for steady meals and protection.
* The operation has hundreds of participants.
* No single creature is moving all the food alone.
* Sable learns that the gangs have been taxing deliveries, stealing civilian rations and starting wars over distribution rights.
* **TWIST 2:** The Architect redirected food away from gang markets to prove that the gangs’ power disappears when workers stop cooperating.
* **STANDALONE PAYOFF:** Sable negotiates the release of emergency food for civilians caught between the gangs.
* That night, every gang leader receives the same invitation:
* **“Transfer control of food distribution. Attend dinner at Depot Seven.”**
* The gangs reject the demand and publicly promise to seize the Depot instead.
* **SERIES CLUE:** Sable keeps her invitation rather than burning it.

## VIDEO 07 — The Impossible Geometry

* **Hook:** Bastet becomes trapped upside down inside a maintenance tunnel while following one of the Architect’s couriers.
* She insists that the rescue team record the incident as “structural failure.”
* Bastet maps every known Drop route.
* Almost all require large animals to kneel, crawl or squeeze through narrow passages.
* At first, she assumes the old city was simply designed badly.
* **TWIST 1:** The narrow sections are new.
* Fresh mortar, recent welds and hidden panels show that someone has modified the city without permits.
* The altered routes surround docks, markets, cold-storage buildings and police records rooms.
* Bastet overlays the passages on a city map.
* They form a second transportation network inside the walls.
* Workers can move messages, keys and small packages without entering gang-controlled streets.
* Bastet discovers a miniature switching station that controls doors and conveyor belts across several blocks.
* It is empty when she arrives.
* **TWIST 2:** The gangs do not truly own the city above ground.
* They are tenants inside a larger logistical system constructed beneath them.
* **STANDALONE PAYOFF:** Bastet uses the hidden controls to reopen a civilian food route that her own Syndicate had previously blocked.
* **SERIES CLUE:** Every junction contains a small silver token stamped with a time and worker number.

## VIDEO 08 — The Ghost in the Machine

* **Hook:** Silas finds microfilm hidden inside a cracked records-hall baseboard.
* It contains a map of the city’s vents, maintenance corridors and delivery schedules.
* Every marked date corresponds to one of his unsolved food cases.
* Silas assumes someone inside the police department leaked patrol information.
* **TWIST 1:** No leak was necessary.
* Police patrol schedules were publicly released as part of a transparency program.
* The Architect merely studied the information better than the police did.
* The map marks an upcoming transfer through a South Docks warehouse.
* Silas organizes a raid with roadblocks, warrants and press coverage.
* The warehouse contains only legal goods and perfect paperwork.
* While Silas searches it, hundreds of delivery trucks are redirected around his roadblocks.
* All of them are forced onto the same unguarded street.
* **TWIST 2:** The Architect planted the map so Silas would create the safest possible route for the real shipment.
* The police raid was not avoided.
* It was used.
* **STANDALONE PAYOFF:** Silas identifies the Depot’s worker network through the redirected vehicle registrations.
* He also discovers that many workers joined voluntarily after gangs stole their wages or threatened their families.
* Silas does not issue arrests.
* Instead, he takes his evidence directly to Depot Seven.
* **SERIES CLUE:** A note appears inside his locked desk:
* **“BRING YOUR WARRANT. THE BOSSES ARE COMING.”**

## VIDEO 09 — The Siege of Depot Seven

* **Hook:** At dawn, the Vulpine Cartel, Lupine Pack and Feline Syndicate march on Depot Seven under a temporary truce.
* They rejected the Architect’s dinner invitation and now intend to seize the food by force.
* Knuckles brings a battering ram.
* The main gate is already open, causing him to fall through it.
* Bastet pretends not to notice.
* Sable joins the raid to protect the civilian shipments she negotiated.
* Silas enters with a warrant, determined to arrest whoever caused the illegal shortages.
* Inside, there are no guards or defensive troops.
* The corridors are spotless, silent and carefully labeled.
* They discover the city’s missing food stacked from floor to ceiling.
* **TWIST 1:** Every crate still carries its original owner’s label and a detailed record of where it will eventually be delivered.
* The food was not collected for the Architect’s personal use.
* It was separated from gang-controlled markets and prepared for neutral distribution.
* Each gang sees crates once stolen from the others.
* Their truce begins collapsing.
* Sable notices that the aisles are guiding everyone toward the same central chamber.
* Silas finds no alternate exit.
* **TWIST 2:** The Depot is not a fortress being invaded.
* It is a meeting hall, and the food was bait that guaranteed every leader would attend.
* **STANDALONE PAYOFF:** The four investigators finally understand that their separate cases were parts of one planned confrontation.
* The doors close behind the gang armies.
* A calm voice announces:
* **“The rejected dinner meeting will now proceed without dinner.”**
* **SERIES CLUE:** Tapping begins behind an ordinary wall panel.

## VIDEO 10 — The Architect Revealed

* **Hook:** Knuckles, Bastet, Sable and Silas are trapped inside Depot Seven with the leaders of all three gangs.
* They search for the giant criminal mastermind controlling the city.
* There is no throne room, private army or enormous beast.
* The tapping continues behind a plain wall panel.
* Knuckles tears it away.
* Behind it is a miniature command center built inside the walls.
* Tiny screens display every dock, market, warehouse and gang-controlled route.
* Hundreds of worker names appear beside scheduled shifts.
* The system is not operated by one creature.
* It is maintained by clerks, drivers, butchers, engineers, former gang members and small animals who can move through the hidden network.
* **TWIST 1:** At the center of the room sits the Architect: a small mouse at a desk made from a silver thimble.
* The silver containers, seals and tokens found throughout the investigation were ordinary thimbles repurposed by his workers.
* Knuckles demands that the mouse come out and fight.
* “I am out,” the mouse replies through a voice amplifier.
* The mouse explains his motive:
* The gangs turned food into territory, protection payments and war.
* Years earlier, they seized the municipal granaries and forced small workers to carry goods through dangerous streets.
* The mouse built a neutral network inside the walls so food could move without gang permission.
* He then bought supplies legally, recruited exploited workers and created a targeted shortage to demonstrate that the gangs controlled nothing without those workers.
* Sable accuses him of endangering civilians.
* The mouse admits that his system became too controlling and that innocent districts suffered during the experiment.
* Silas produces his warrant.
* The mouse produces complete ledgers documenting every gang theft, murder, bribe and illegal food tax.
* Every file is ready to be broadcast publicly.
* **TWIST 2:** The surrender contract does not transfer the food supply to the mouse.
* It transfers all docks, granaries and delivery routes to an independent municipal distribution board controlled by workers and monitored by Silas.
* The gangs must surrender their food monopolies, repay stolen wages and stop taxing civilian shipments.
* If they refuse, the workers will permanently strike, the evidence will be released, and the gangs will leave the Depot with no food, money or public support.
* Knuckles cannot punch the worker network.
* Bastet cannot outmaneuver evidence that implicates her entire Syndicate.
* Sable cannot broker around a system that no longer needs gang permission.
* One by one, the leaders sign.
* **STANDALONE PAYOFF:** The Architect defeats the city’s strongest gangs without personally throwing a punch, but he is also forced to surrender sole control of his system.
* Silas appoints Sable as civilian negotiator, Bastet as logistics adviser and Knuckles as security—under strict supervision.
* The mouse keeps one seat on the worker board.
* **FINAL SHOT:** Knuckles struggles to use the mouse-sized pen.
* The mouse rolls him a full-sized one and says:
* **“I planned for that.”**
