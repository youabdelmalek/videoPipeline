VIDEO 01 - The Vanishing Salmon
- Hook: The manifest says forty salmon crates. The locked dock cage holds thirty-nine.
- Sable brokered this shipment; her commission and her neck are both on the block.
- Seals intact, cameras clean, two sober guards who agree on absolutely nothing.
- The cage was never opened all night. Everyone assumes a miracle heist anyway.
- Twist 1: The upstate loading ticket also says thirty-nine. The cage was honest; the invoice lied.
- The fish was skimmed at the source; the "locked-room miracle" is a padded invoice.
- The receiving clerk who signed for forty has taken sudden paid leave, and he is terrified.
- Sable corners him. He whispers only: "You don't say no to the Drop."
- Continuity clue: A silver thimble sits dead center of the empty cage, like a calling card.
- Somewhere in the wall, a rhythmic tapping. Sable blames the plumbing.
- Twist 2 / Payoff: Crate thirteen resurfaces at the Cartel's own restaurant, batch tags intact, priced triple.
- The "thief" sold the Cartel its own fish back. The heist was a price hike.
- Standalone payoff: Sable gets it — nothing was stolen tonight; the whole city just paid a tax.
- Final detail: On her desk, an unsigned reorder form for forty crates, exact change enclosed.

VIDEO 02 - The Wolf's Paranoia
- Hook: Knuckles wins the street fight, claims the block — then abandons it before dawn.
- The Pack's hidden cache on that block was already empty. He won a worthless corner.
- He wants someone to hit. There is no one. He schedules drills against a sound.
- His crew mocks him — until his prized bone vanishes from a locked den.
- It comes back the next night: polished, displayed on the top shelf.
- He rigs tripwires and bells. Morning: nothing triggered, but every wire is re-knotted — neater.
- Twist 1: The only thing missing is his territorial map. The intruder isn't robbing him; it's studying him.
- A Vulpine defector shows him the same: polished locks, improved traps, missing map.
- Then the shortage hits. The Pack owns more streets than ever and eats less.
- Twist 2 / Payoff: His map comes back — corrected, every lost block redrawn along supply lines, not streets.
- Standalone payoff: Knuckles finally sees it: he isn't losing a fight, he's losing a delivery schedule.
- You can't punch a supply chain.
- Continuity clue: His den's deadbolt was locked from the inside; the only key never left his pocket.
- Final detail: Tapping behind the drywall. Knuckles posts a guard to listen and take notes.

VIDEO 03 - The Syndicate's Code
- Hook: Bastet opens her third intercepted Drop: no contraband, just a wax-sealed square of numbers.
- The Syndicate is bleeding the fish market; she needs the Old Ways to bite again.
- Her codebreakers — two toms in reading glasses — fail all week. She cracks it in an hour.
- It isn't a cipher. It's a delivery schedule.
- Twist 1: The schedule is the Syndicate's own — listing shipments her office never approved.
- Her captains obey it anyway: the phantom routes are faster, cheaper, always on time.
- She issues a contradicting order as a test. The gang follows "the memo" instead.
- Her lieutenant apologizes — to the memo.
- Her spies lift matching parchments from Vulpine and Lupine dens.
- Twist 2 / Payoff: The three schedules interlock like gears, arranged so no two gangs ever share a dock.
- Standalone payoff: This isn't espionage; it's crowd control. The Syndicate doesn't have a rival — it has a manager.
- Continuity clue: Every Drop canister is a wax-sealed silver thimble; Bastet blames some jeweler's guild.
- Final detail: One last square slides from a vent grate — blank, except for her name.

VIDEO 04 - The Detective's Dead End
- Hook: Eleven food markets collapse in one week, and Silas's evidence wall is bare.
- No break-ins, no transfers, no suspects. His whiteboard is just receipts.
- He digs: every vanished shipment was legally purchased, fair price, cash on delivery.
- Twist 1: There is no crime wave, because there is no crime — just perfect ledgers and empty shelves.
- The buyers are forty shell names spending one brick of cash; the serial numbers run in sequence.
- One wallet bought this city's entire food supply, politely.
- He tails a pickup: prepaid catering van, sealed instructions, destination the Depot.
- Three market guards swore they saw nothing. Under oath: nothing, again.
- Twist 2 / Payoff: The guards weren't lying. The food left in daylight, as scheduled pickups, receipts in hand.
- Standalone payoff: Silas isn't hunting a thief; he's hunting an accountant with unlimited change.
- Continuity clue: An evidence clerk tags a silver thimble from the market floor as "costume jewelry."
- Final detail: A rustle follows him down the records hall. He writes it off as pigeons.

VIDEO 05 - The Unholy Alliance
- Hook: Knuckles and Bastet, sworn enemies, share a rooftop and one ambush plan.
- Target: the midnight shipment that has never once run late.
- They stop the truck: driver asleep, unhurt, cargo present — restacked, labeled, better organized.
- The route sheet is improved too. Knuckles is offended on the truck's behalf.
- Twist 1: The manifest lists their ambush as a pre-printed twenty-minute stop: "INSPECTION."
- They were expected. Their free will has a time slot.
- The driver recalls one detail: coffee waiting at the weigh station, his name on it.
- They hunt the mole: three fake plans fed to three lieutenants. None get used.
- Twist 2 / Payoff: Bastet finds the channel — batch stamps on their own ration tins, positions mailed by themselves.
- Standalone payoff: Not watched — cc'd. Bastet, quietly: "We're not prey. We're inventory."
- The loading bay seals at midnight — posted hours, nothing personal.
- Continuity clue: Tapping inside the walls runs in shifts, like a change of guard.
- Final detail: Doors open at six sharp; the vent drops an unsigned note: "DELAY LOGGED. APOLOGIES."

VIDEO 06 - The Great Hunger
- Hook: Citywide bulletin: total protein shortage. By sunset, gangs are mugging hot dog carts.
- Sable's brokerage is collapsing; nobody pays a middleman for nothing.
- She pitches a Vulpine–Feline truce; both sides demand snacks at the table first.
- Meat prices triple, then vanish. Even the black market's shelves echo.
- Twist 1: The pigeons are fat. The strays are glossy. Food is moving — just not to gangs.
- She tails a plump stray through back lanes to a grate her snout won't fit.
- The long way around leads to the Depot, its yard stacked with everyone's missing dinners.
- At night she watches silent figures turn looters away, never showing their faces.
- Twist 2 / Payoff: The "guards" are butchers, clerks, ex-gang muscle — paid in meal vouchers.
- Standalone payoff: No army needed; hunger does the hiring. The city is being starved into an appointment.
- That night, every gang boss receives the same dinner invitation. No host is named.
- Continuity clue: A silver thimble snags the Depot's fence mesh, polished by daily handling.
- Final detail: At the peace table, every seat fills except the head chair. Everyone waits for it.

VIDEO 07 - The Impossible Geometry
- Hook: Bastet is stuck in a maintenance tunnel — upside down, dignified, declining assistance.
- She chased a courier's route; the route fit, she didn't.
- Extraction takes an hour. She bills the Syndicate for "equipment failure."
- She maps every Drop site: none reachable without kneeling, squeezing, or dislocating something.
- Twist 1: The mortar is fresh, the welds new. The city wasn't built cramped — it was edited.
- Planning records show no permits, no work orders — yet the renovations are flawless.
- She files a complaint with City Planning just to watch them find nothing. They find nothing.
- Overlaying the edits: every narrowed passage guards a food choke point — docks, markets, cold storage.
- Twist 2 / Payoff: The renovations connect into one network; the whole city has become somebody's floor plan.
- Standalone payoff: Bastet sees it — the Syndicate isn't a power in this city. It's a tenant.
- Continuity clue: A silver thimble hangs by every edited junction, like a foreman's punch-clock token.
- Final detail: From inside the pipe behind her, three polite taps — like a door held open.

VIDEO 08 - The Ghost in the Machine
- Hook: In a cracked records-hall baseboard, Silas finds microfilm: a map of the city's vents.
- It's annotated with times and dates, and every date matches one of his dead-end cases.
- He charts the pattern: every impossible transfer happened inside a patrol gap.
- Twist 1: No leak needed — the patrol rotation was published for budget transparency. The ghost reads newspapers.
- The map marks one more convergence: Thursday, a South Docks warehouse.
- He stakes it out; figures move in eerie unison — a shift change doing stretches.
- Thursday, he raids: roadblocks, warrants, full press. Inside: legal goods, clean paperwork.
- Twist 2 / Payoff: His roadblocks funneled every truck onto one street — the shipment's street. The raid was the getaway.
- Standalone payoff: The ghost doesn't dodge the system; it books appointments with it.
- He reports none of this. He starts carrying exact change.
- Continuity clue: The map's corner bears a wax seal pressed from a silver thimble.
- Final detail: His locked desk drawer scratches. Inside, a note: "THANK YOU FOR YOUR COOPERATION."

VIDEO 09 - The Siege of the Depot
- Hook: Dawn. Three gangs, one truce, one battering ram. The Depot falls today.
- The ram swings; the gate is already open. Knuckles falls through, dignity intact-ish.
- No guards, no mercenaries — corridors spotless, silent, organized.
- They find the hoard: the city's missing food, floor to ceiling.
- Twist 1: Every crate bears its original owner's tag. Nothing here was kept — it was filed.
- Each crate carries an itemized storage invoice, payable on collection.
- The truce lasts four minutes; a crate of herring ends it.
- Bastet studies the layout: aisles funnel inward; none lead back to the gate.
- Twist 2 / Payoff: Not a vault — a muster point. The food was bait, and the bait worked.
- Standalone payoff: They didn't storm anything. They answered a summons.
- Continuity clue: Behind one ordinary wall panel, faint rhythmic tapping. Only Knuckles hears it.
- Final detail: The gates seal; a calm recorded voice thanks them for coming. "The meeting begins shortly."

VIDEO 10 - The Architect Revealed
- Hook: Trapped in the Depot's dark heart, Knuckles and Bastet hunt the owner of that voice.
- No throne room, no army, no human — just one ordinary wall panel, still tapping.
- Knuckles tears it off: light, a room the size of a lunchbox, a city inside the wall.
- Miniature boulevards of wire, screens like postage stamps, ledgers stacked like confetti.
- Every screen shows a gang's territory; every ledger holds their whole "war," itemized, initialed.
- Twist 1: The Architect is not human, not a beast. The throne is a silver thimble — and occupied.
- The camera sinks past the crates: one small mouse, seated, patient as a judge.
- Knuckles roars for it to come out and fight. It is out. It was never anywhere else.
- Bastet whispers the evidence backward: the Drops, the seals, the tapping — orders, tapped nightly.
- The mouse sets one paw on a lever.
- Twist 2 / Payoff: Every steel door in the Depot slams shut; the giants are locked in their victory.
- Beside the lever waits a surrender contract — and a full-size pen, rolled to Knuckles' feet.
- Standalone payoff: The city's boss never threw a punch. He signed things. Now they will too.
- Continuity clue: The thimbles at every scene — his seal, his cup, his throne.
- Above the door, a faded sign: MUNICIPAL GRANARY NO. 7.
- Final detail: The mouse stares into the camera and squeaks once. It sounds like a laugh.
